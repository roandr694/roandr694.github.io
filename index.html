About

I'm Rodrigo, an Electrical and Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Southern California, with an expected graduation in May 2027. My research focuses on robot learning, manipulation, and humanoid robots.

At the Physical Superintelligence (PSI) Lab, I work on teleoperation, demonstration data collection, and imitation learning for robotic manipulation. At the Dynamics Robotics and Control Laboratory, I contribute to humanoid hardware, inverse kinematics, and experiments with learned walking policies.

I like working across the boundary between software and hardware: assembling a robot, building the tools to collect data, and testing how a learned policy behaves on a physical system.

Beyond research, I co-founded Projeto++, an initiative providing free robotics education to public school students in Brazil. My personal projects also explore embedded systems, renewable energy, and optics.
