PRIMUS, a wheel-legged biped

A self-balancing robot with closed five-bar legs that I designed in Fusion 360 and built. Its CAD becomes a MuJoCo model, a model-based controller balances it, and learned policies add robustness and hops over steps and gaps.

2026 / Optimal control · Numerical methods

Constrained Trajectory Optimization Under Uncertainty

A simulation-based planner for nonlinear dynamics with delayed inputs. I combine a compartment model, stratified parameter sampling and derivative-free search to track a reference while enforcing modeled limits.
